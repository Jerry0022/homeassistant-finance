## What this round must do

Build on the Budget-Tank (keep its look: tank with fill level, dashed plan line, pots grid, HA-like cards, accent #03a9f4 / tank blues). The user asks how the **different perspectives ("Sichtweisen")** are realised, grounded in the requirements in `docs/dev/product-concept.md` and `docs/dev/visual-round-handoff.md` (read both). Round 1 ignored them. Perspectives to make visible:

1. **Person perspective — mine / yours / ours** (concept § Household and privacy, § Ownership): N persons, optimised for two (demo: Anna, Ben). Joint accounts and the plan fully visible to all; another member's personal accounts show **totals only**. Ownership chain: account owner default → matched plan position → user correction/rule.
2. **Real account vs. virtual pots** (concept § Goals and reserves): pots behave like separate accounts, view switch back to the real account, coverage indicator; shortfall warns and names top-up amount.
3. **Time perspective — rhythms** (concept § Rhythms): Glance, Week (calendar week, digest + review inbox max 10, owner first then category, points earned), Cycle (salary cycle, close: plan vs actual per position and person, settle-up), Year (contracts, annual cost, notice deadlines, benchmark with statistic/source/year, Regelbedarf floor), Event (exceptions only, routed to the person concerned).
4. **Surface perspective — form factors** (handoff § Form factors): phone, wall tablet, HA dashboard card, desktop panel; every interaction touch-capable. The panel today is admin-only; a non-admin household view is required.

Constraints: no guilt tone, savings hints neutral and never addressed at a person; only exceptions notify; plan sign: expense positive.

## Structure (suggestion — adapt if a better cut exists, explain it in the intro)

One visual topic per design set: this round's designs are **topics**, not alternatives — each shows one perspective in the Budget-Tank language, so set `data-design-set="topics"` (no favourite star). Overview attributes on each design (motto, three traits, icon, colour not accent blue). Suggested designs:

- **d1 „Wer schaut?" (Person)** — screens: Haushalt (ours: shared tank + both persons' small tanks), Annas Sicht (her tank in front, Ben's personal account as total only, with a visible "nur Summe" marker), Korrektur (a booking whose owner is changed: "für uns / nur ich / immer so" with reach choice this booking / merchant / pattern).
- **d2 „Topf oder Konto" (Pots)** — screens: Töpfe-Sicht (pots as accounts), Echtes Konto (switch, coverage bar: pots sum vs real balance), Unterdeckung (warning with top-up amount, neutral wording).
- **d3 „Wann schaust du?" (Rhythm)** — screens: Woche (digest + inbox 1/10, points progress), Zyklus-Abschluss (plan vs actual per position and person, settle-up), Jahr (contract timeline across the year with notice deadlines, annual cost; one benchmark row labelled "Median · Destatis EVS · 2023" and the Regelbedarf floor), Ereignis (one notification: "Doppelte Abbuchung Netflix — an Ben").
- **d4 „Wo schaust du?" (Surface)** — screens: HA-Dashboard-Karte (compact tank card in an HA dashboard grid), Wand-Tablet (landscape, large tank + both persons, touch-sized), Desktop-Panel (sidebar + tank + details).

Wire every design's screens with `data-screen-link` (chips / buttons). A perspective switcher (chip row "Haushalt · Anna · Ben", "Töpfe · Konto") should be clickable and navigate between the matching screens.

## Views (mode widens to mixed — the user asks "wie realisieren")

Add ONE `data-view-kind="decision"` view, `data-view="r2-sicht-quelle"`, nav label „Wer bestimmt die Sicht?": how the person perspective is chosen. Alternatives (legacy bi-state markup exactly as in `templates-design-views.md` § View kind decision, each with its `-note` textarea): (a) automatisch aus dem angemeldeten HA-Benutzer (HA user → person mapping, set in the setup wizard) — **recommended, preselected include**; (b) Umschalter im Panel, jeder darf jede Sicht wählen (privacy still enforced by data: totals only); (c) pro Dashboard-Karte fest konfiguriert (card option `person:`), for the wall tablet that has no personal login; (d) Kombination a + c (login decides in the panel, card config decides on shared devices). Since (d) combines, preselect include on (a) and (d)? Keep it simple: these are independent enough — preselect include on a and c, discard on b, and explain d is a+c together in the prose (so drop d). Orthogonal to the designs (never names a design).

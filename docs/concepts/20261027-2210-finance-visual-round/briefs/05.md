## What round 5 must do

### 1. Design system screen (new, first screen of the design: `r5-ds` „Design-System")
A one-page token sheet the rest of the round visibly uses — replacing ad-hoc colours (incl. the black Eilmeldung background): surfaces (page, card, raised, notification), text levels, accent (#03a9f4 family), **status semantics** (im Plan / knapp / drüber — and the ±10 % rule: ≤ linear+10 % = ok, above = drüber), person colours (Ben, Anna, Haushalt), desktop-only marker (purple), type scale, radius, spacing, the notification style (Eilmeldung pill + panel, NOT black — e.g. elevated surface with a coloured status edge), chips, buttons, the progress/tank component. Dark-mode note optional. Keep it compact and visual (swatches, specimens), not a wall of text.

### 2. Home screens per horizon (zoom stays: Heute · Woche · **Zyklus** · Jahr — rename Monat → Zyklus everywhere)
Strict separation of what belongs to which horizon (from the feedback):
- **Heute** (`r5-s1` Person, `r5-s2` Haushalt): only today. One calm, simple answer: im Plan? + „heute frei" (total) + pots as a clean list with „heute frei" each, coloured by the ±10 % rule. NO Soll-Linie/time axis, NO Als Nächstes, NO „unkategorisiert" line (the star suffices), NO Wusstest du schon, NO Rücklagen, NO mini-glasses per person, NO accounts (persons and Haushalt only). Top bar: freshness icon, Eilmeldung pill (new design-system style), star with badge. Redesign the **Person ⇄ Haushalt switch** so it looks intentional (e.g. a segmented header with avatars or a clear tab-like title row with swipe affordance) — show it on both screens.
- **Woche** (`r5-s3`): the box is dominated by the verdict („knapp über Soll" etc.). Show the week's linear daily budget vs actual per day (day bars with an over-day highlighted), the **carry-over** from last week (+/−, only within the current cycle, e.g. „+18 € aus KW 43 mitgenommen") and the aggregated unspent amount so far. Plus „wird noch abgebucht diese Woche" (sum + top 3 incl. vorgemerkte with a „vorgemerkt" label inline).
- **Zyklus** (`r5-s4`): the cycle (from the highest income, per the rule below) with the time aspect (Soll-Linie belongs here), **Als Nächstes** (top 3 + mehr anzeigen, vorgemerkte inline with label), **Wusstest du schon?** (Haushalt), **Rücklagen** (Notgroschen, Urlaub …). Show the cycle range („Zyklus 28.10. – 27.11. · ab Gehalt Anna").
- **Jahr** (`r5-s5`, can be light): year view with Wusstest du schon (year flavour) and Rücklagen progress; contracts only as an entry, not detailed.
- Keep `r4-s4` Eilmeldungen-Panel (restyled to the design system) as `r5-s6`, Karteikarten as `r5-s7` with a **new, thematic counter** (finance / tidying-up theme — e.g. sorted coins stacking up, a ledger page filling, puzzle of the overview coming together; pick one and say why in an improved-line) and **7 cards per point**, Feier `r5-s8`, Datenstand `r5-s9` — mostly carried over, restyled to the design system.

### 3. Desktop (`r5-s10` Desktop Zyklus, optionally `r5-s11` Desktop Woche)
Desktop puts the **analysis use cases** in focus per horizon: Zyklus → optimisation (where the cycle deviates, why, what to change — multi-dimensional per the standing principle in `briefs/later.md`), Jahr → optimisation, Woche → budget adjustments (shift budget between pots, accept carry-over). Accounts can be broken down here (person → DKB, PayPal; Haushalt → Gemeinschaftskonto). Desktop-only controls carry the purple marker and may sit inline in the boxes that also exist on mobile.

### Cycle rule (show it in the Zyklus screen as a small „so wird der Zyklus bestimmt" info, and use it in the numbers)
A person's cycle runs from their earliest highest income to the next one. Haushalt: the highest income sets the cycle start, unless another person's highest income falls within the 7 days before it — then that earlier one is the start.

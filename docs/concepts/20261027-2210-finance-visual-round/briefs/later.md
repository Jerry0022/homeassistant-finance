## Standing principle for every analysis view (user, 2026-10-28)

Analyses must not be one-dimensional "here is a statistic, look at it and decide". Not complicated for its own sake, but each one must deliver more than the raw number: combine dimensions (time trend × plan × benchmark × owner × fixed/variable), say what it means, show where action is needed and where not, and offer the concrete next step (a guided path, a saved rule, a plan adjustment, a contract to cancel). The goal is insight and an improvement path, not a chart.

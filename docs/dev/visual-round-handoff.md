# Visual / mobile round — hand-off

Input for the next concept round, which decides how everything in `product-concept.md` is shown.
Purpose and interactions are settled; this round decides presentation only.

## Form factors

Phone, wall tablet, HA dashboard card, desktop panel. Every interaction needs a touch path.

## Surfaces to design

- **Glance card**: status, free to spend, next debits, freshness stamp, pending apart.
- **Weekly check**: digest, review inbox (owner first, then category, max. 10), points earned.
- **Cycle close**: plan vs. actual per position and person, settle-up.
- **Mine / yours / ours**: presentation optimised for two persons, N possible.
- **Contracts and subscriptions**: timeline "relative to x / y / z in the year", charts, notice
  deadlines.
- **Virtual pots**: shown like separate accounts, with a switch to the real account and a
  coverage indicator.
- **Benchmarks**: every figure with statistic (median/mean), source and year; the floor
  (Regelbedarf) visible.
- **Setup wizard**: a guided flow that resumes until completed once.
- **Rules and corrections**: reach choice, tolerances, "why this category".

## Constraints carried over

- No guilt tone; savings hints neutral, never addressed at a person.
- Personal accounts of another member show totals only.
- Only exceptions notify; everything else waits for the digest.
- Concept pages are authored in the user's language (German).
